The Epistemic Architecture Behind Veriscribe

A deterministic, cross-document fact extraction and reconciliation framework designed to eliminate hallucination in high-stakes domain workflows.

01 /

The Problem with Unstructured Corporate Knowledge

Unstructured enterprise disclosures, scientific pre-prints, and regulatory filings contain overlapping, complementary, or directly contradictory factual propositions that traditional semantic search and generation systems conflate or fail to audit. Standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) relies on probabilistic embedding distances, treating textual proximity as factual truth.

When an enterprise operates across disparate quarterly filings, board prospectuses, and compliance mandates, subtle variations in dates, financial covenants, and fiscal baselines become invisible epistemological liabilities. Without structural lineage, large language models synthesize coherent hallucinations from incompatible fragments, rendering downstream audit trails non-verifiable.

02 /

Atomic Fact Extraction and Exact Grounding

Veriscribe replaces probabilistic document chunking with a deterministic parse pipeline. Dense PDF paragraphs, tables, and charts are parsed into standalone, atomic factual assertions, each bound to exact coordinate vectors, bounding boxes, and verbatim citations linked permanently to the canonical page source.

Canonical Token Offsets INDEX REF: DOC-04 • P.18 • §2.1

"Total Q3 capital expenditure confirmed at $42.8M within primary data infrastructure facilities."

Each extracted entity possesses an immutable cryptographic hash based on its character span and layout topology. This ensures analysts can inspect the raw evidentiary substrate in the adjacent viewer without context degradation or token transposition.

03 /

Graph-Based Reconciliation & Dialectical Reasoning

Extracted claims are indexed into a hypergraph alignment engine that continuously cross-references factual assertions across corporate corpora. Rather than applying subjective heuristic scoring, Veriscribe clusters parallel assertions into four mathematically governed epistemic categories:

[✓] Corroborated CONF: 99.4%

Claims validated across multiple independent canonical filings with identical semantic invariants.

[✕] Contradicted DELTA > 0.0

Irreconcilable factual tensions between sources, indexed into a comparative typographic ledger.

[⚖] Reconciled Divergence TIME-VARIANT

Apparent discrepancies resolved by temporal precedence, revision filings, or scope qualifiers.

[?] Ambiguous INSUFFICIENT DATA

Assertions lacking verifiable evidentiary anchors or containing under-specified parameters.

Dialectical consensus is evaluated through strict symbolic logic trees. Analysts are presented with verifiable claim chains rather than aggregated text summaries, enabling rapid evidentiary verification in high-stakes environments.
